About the Foundation

Thinkers Foundation is an independent review devoted to economics, moral politics, and philosophy. It exists for a single purpose: to put thinking itself into public view, in the conviction that an argument is worth more to a reader than a conclusion.

Most writing that reaches a general audience arrives with its reasoning removed. Positions are announced, figures are cited, and the intervening labour, the definitions accepted, the alternatives weighed and set aside, the concessions that a serious case is obliged to make, is left on the workshop floor. The Foundation publishes the labour. A piece earns its place here by showing how it arrived somewhere, and by remaining intelligible to a reader who has never opened a textbook on the subject.

The review is not a personal blog and does not function as one. It carries no proprietor's column, and no writer holds a standing claim on its pages. What appears is selected on the merits of the argument alone, and pieces that dissent from work already published are welcome on precisely the same terms as those that extend it.

Alongside the essays, the Foundation maintains the Political Thought Guide, a standing work of reference covering six eras of political philosophy from the Greek polis to the present. The guide is descriptive rather than argumentative, and is kept deliberately separate from the essays so that neither is mistaken for the other.

Submissions

Essays, review pieces, and research notes are read year-round. There is no fixed length, though most published work runs between fifteen hundred and four thousand words, and no requirement of academic affiliation. Clarity is preferred to credentials, and a well-defended minority position to a fashionable one loosely held.

Send work, in the body of the message or as an attachment, to teamthinkersfoundation@gmail.com. Correspondence, corrections, and objections may be sent to the same address, and substantive corrections are published.
